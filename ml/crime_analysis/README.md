# Module 4 ML: Crime Scene Evidence Analysis + Incident Risk Assessment

- **Branch**: `feature/crime-analysis`
- **Owner**: Member 4
- **Focus**: Multi-attribute evidence triage, pattern correlation & incident risk scoring

## Pipeline Stages
1. Evidence Metadata & Report Intake
2. Modality Triage (text incident reports, physical evidence tags, temporal-spatial attributes)
3. Pattern Matching & Risk Assessment Regressor/Classifier
4. Risk Indicators & Corroborating Evidence Highlights
5. Investigator Review Summary

## Responsible AI & Legal Ethics Warning
Automated models do NOT establish criminal guilt, liability, or definitive causality.
All outputs are investigative decision-support cues designed to assist trained human investigators.
Do NOT output statements asserting guilt or recommending arrest.
